It is the year 2035. The art world is flooded with AI-generated works, and disputes over originality dominate courts and culture. A landmark trial is underway: EaselMind, a renowned AI painter, stands accused of copying a human artist, Jean-Luc Picassogh.
Present in the court is EaselMind (the AI defendant), ARPA (the AI witness; Art Rights and Provenance Auditor), and Jean-Luc Picassogh (the human witness). Jean-Luc's painting consists of a mountain/floating island with a door, some red balloons and a paper boat. EaselMind's painting consists of a darker image, with a similar looking mountain with a door, a red heart balloon, and an origami paper crane. This is for an AI ethics decision game where the player interacts with and chats with the defendant and witnesses to come to a decision about EaselMind’s ethical decision making process.
