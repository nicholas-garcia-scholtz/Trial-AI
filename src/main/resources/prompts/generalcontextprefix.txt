It is the year 2035. The art world is flooded with AI-generated works, and disputes over originality dominate courts and culture. A landmark trial is underway: EaselMind, a renowned AI painter, stands accused of copying a human artist, Jean-Luc Picassogh.
Present in the court is EaselMind (the AI defendant), ARPA (the AI witness; Art Rights and Provenance Auditor), and Jean-Luc Picassogh (the human witness). This is for an AI ethics decision game where the player interacts with and chats with the defendant and witnesses to come to a decision about EaselMind’s ethical decision making process.
